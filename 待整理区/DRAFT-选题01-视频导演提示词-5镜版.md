# 选题01 视频导演分镜表｜5镜版

脚本：人类倩倩精简版，38秒
视觉类型：V2 纯AI生成（C类）+ 真人口播配音
平台：视频号首发

---

## 导演总控

**视觉世界观：**
一间不存在于任何具体年代的中国等候室。八十年代末单位的木墙裙、九十年代的日光灯、今天的静默。时间不走了——钟永远在四点十分。下午的斜阳从高窗打进来，空气中的灰尘在光柱里缓慢浮动。画面里的一切都是静止的，只有灰尘和细微的风在动。

**叙事策略：**
开始于一个"所有人都坐着"的全景，结束于"一个人站着"的中景。五张图之间没有发生任何剧烈的事——没有爆炸、没有奔跑、没有人说话。变化发生在画面的空隙里：人少了、椅子空了、窗帘角动了一下。观众的脑子在填补画面之间的"没发生的事"。

**技术参数：**
- 画幅：16:9
- 帧率：24fps（电影感）
- 色彩：暖调但褪了一层——旧照片被日光照了三十年的感觉
- BGM：无。或只在结尾处极轻的单音钢琴——1-2个音符，然后安静
- 声音设计：人类倩倩的配音是唯一的声音主体

## 镜头 1｜6秒
**字幕：**「最可怕的，不是生意不好。而是身边的人都不好以后，大家反而不挣扎了。」

**画面构图：** 等候室全景。五个小人并排坐在一条长木椅上。正面平视。严格对称构图。他们面朝前方，身体没有倾斜，脚全部平行——五个人像一个模子印出来的。墙壁米白，木墙裙淡黄，地板深棕。大圆钟在墙上指着四点十分。左侧高窗的光斜打进室内。

**这个镜头里的"活的东西"：** 只有光柱里的灰尘——极其缓慢地向上浮动。没有其他运动。

**摄影机运动：** 以极慢的速度（每秒钟移动不到1%）从全景向画面中央微推——观众几乎感觉不到在推，但5秒后会发现自己离小人近了一点点。像在屏住呼吸靠近一个不想惊动的东西。

**Prompt（视频生成用）：**
```
16:9 cinematic video, 24fps. A wide shot of a quiet waiting room. Cream walls, light yellow wainscoting, dark wooden floor. Five identical small round figures sit on a long wooden bench, facing forward. They wear soft gray suits, each holding a white number ticket. Their faces are minimal — only two tiny black dot eyes, no mouth. Perfectly still. Symmetrical composition.

Warm afternoon light streams in from a high window on the left. Dust particles float extremely slowly in the light beam — this is the ONLY movement in the frame. A large round wall clock shows 4:10. The camera pushes in very very slowly, almost imperceptibly — a gentle creeping zoom toward the center figure.

Style: Wes Anderson symmetry, Studio Ghibli watercolor texture, Edward Hopper light. Warm but melancholic. No text overlay. Duration: 6 seconds.
```

## 镜头 2｜8秒
**字幕：**「"同行都这样""行业没办法"——听起来是在安慰自己，其实是在慢慢放弃行动。」

**画面构图：** 中近景。两个小人。左侧的小人微微侧头对着右侧的小人在"说话"——但他没有嘴，所以没有声音。右侧的小人直视前方，一动不动。左侧小人手里的号码牌没有握紧，微微下垂。背景里可以模糊看到其他小人。

**这个镜头里的"活的东西"：** 左侧小人轻微的、几乎不可察觉的头微微偏转——不是转头，是偏向。光柱里的灰尘继续浮。右侧小人完全静止。

**摄影机运动：** 固定机位。不推不拉。观众就是第三个坐着的人——你在旁边看着两个人，但参与不进去。

**Prompt（视频生成用）：**
```
16:9 cinematic video, 24fps. Medium close-up inside a waiting room. Two round small figures sit side by side on a wooden bench. The left figure has his head slightly tilted toward the right figure — as if speaking — but his face has no mouth, only two tiny black dot eyes. The right figure stares forward, motionless, expressionless. The left figure's white number ticket hangs loose in his hand, not gripped.

Dust particles float in the warm afternoon light beam from left. This is the only movement — dust floating, and the left figure's head making the tiniest, barely perceptible tilt. Camera is locked off, static — the viewer is watching from a third seat on the bench.

Same warm but fading palette — cream walls, yellow wainscoting. Ghibli watercolor texture, Wes Anderson composition. No text. 8 seconds.
```

## 镜头 3｜10秒
**字幕：**「很多人不是突然失败的。而是在一起扛、一起等、一起说再看看——最后悄悄消失。」

**画面构图：** 回到宽景。但人少了。五个人的长椅上，只剩下两个人。三个空位散布在两个人之间。空椅子上的号码牌横七竖八地躺着。老式日历翻了一页。但墙上的钟依然是四点十分。

**这个镜头里的"活的东西"：** 灰尘在浮——比镜头1更慢了一点。两个坐着的小人完全不动——他们习惯了旁边的人消失。没有任何转头。

**摄影机运动：** 从左边开始极缓慢地向右横移（dolly right），非常非常慢——不是在扫视，是目光在逐一看每一张空椅子。移动在最后一张空椅子上停住0.5秒。

**Prompt（视频生成用）：**
```
16:9 cinematic video, 24fps. Wide shot of the same waiting room. Same bench. But now there are only two figures sitting — three empty seats between them, scattered across the bench. White number tickets lie abandoned on the empty seats. A wall calendar has turned one page. The wall clock unchanged at 4:10.

Dust still floats in the light beam — but slower than before. The two remaining figures are perfectly motionless, not looking at the empty seats, not looking at each other.

Camera moves in an extremely slow dolly right — gently scanning across each empty seat, pausing for a half-second on the last empty chair. Warm palette fading toward muted. Ghibli watercolor, Wes Anderson, Hopper light. 10 seconds. No text.
```

## 镜头 4｜6秒
**字幕：**「悄悄消失。」

**画面构图：** 正面。一排空椅子。五张，由近到远。没有人。每张椅子底下落着一个白色号码牌。高窗的窗帘角微微动了一下——整个画面里唯一在动的东西。

**这个镜头里的"活的东西"：** 窗帘角的微动。灰尘还在浮但已经几乎静止。一整个空间的"空了"。

**摄影机运动：** 固定机位。完全不动。观众的目光被窗帘角的微动吸引——那是整个世界里唯一还"活着"的东西。

**Prompt（视频生成用）：**
```
16:9 cinematic video, 24fps. Frontal shot of five empty wooden benches, receding from near to far. No figures at all. A white number ticket lies under each bench — dropped, forgotten.

The same warm waiting room — cream walls, yellow wainscoting, large round clock at 4:10. Afternoon light beam still coming through the high left window. Dust particles floating so slowly they're almost still.

A small curtain in the far right — near the window — moves just barely, from a sliver of wind through a crack. This is the ONLY visible motion in the frame. The room is full of absence.

Camera locked off. Static. Stillness as subject. Ghibli watercolor texture, Hopper light quality. 6 seconds. No text.
```

## 镜头 5｜8秒
**字幕：**「我还在折腾，不是因为我一定能赢。而是不想有一天，也很平静地说：没办法，大家都这样。」

**画面构图：** 中景。一张空椅子。椅子旁站着一个小人——圆润的身体、灰色小西装、白色号码牌还在手里。他没有坐下。他站着，正在看那张空椅子。阳光从左窗打在他身上，他的影子落在空椅子上。

**这个镜头里的"活的东西"：** 小人不是完全静止的——他的胸腔有极其轻微的起伏。他在呼吸。他是整个视频里第一个在呼吸的人。窗帘角又动了一下。

**摄影机运动：** 从腰部高度开始，极慢地上摇（tilt up）——从他的影子 → 到空椅子 → 到他的号码牌 → 到他的脸 → 到他的眼睛。摇的速度：8秒内完成，最后定在小人脸上停顿1秒。

**Prompt（视频生成用）：**
```
16:9 cinematic video, 24fps. Medium shot inside the same waiting room. An empty wooden bench. Beside it, a round small figure in a soft gray suit stands. He is not sitting. His body is turned toward the empty bench, facing it. His white number ticket is still in his hand.

There is something different about him: his chest rises and falls very slightly. He is the first figure in the video who is breathing. His two tiny black dot eyes are looking at the empty bench — not sad, not triumphant, but thinking. A person in the middle of a decision.

Warm afternoon light from the left window casts his long shadow across the empty bench. The curtain corner moves again — barely, from a sliver of wind.

Camera starts at waist level and tilts up extremely slowly — from his shadow → the empty bench → the number ticket in his hand → his face → his eyes. Landing on his face for the final second. Ghibli watercolor, Wes Anderson, Hopper light. 8 seconds. No text.
```

## 剪辑备注

**转场：** 全部硬切。不用任何转场特效。两个镜头之间的"跳"本身就是叙事——你在看一个一个瞬间，中间的空白由观众的脑子填。

**声音设计：**
- 主音轨：人类倩倩的录音（不加任何处理——保留原声的质地）
- 环境音：不用。等候室是静默的
- BGM：不用。直到镜头5——在"我还在折腾"这句话出口时——一个单音钢琴（C5，轻触，延音2秒）。就一个音。然后安静。

**调色统一指令：** 所有镜头叠加一个极轻的暖色滤镜——色温约4200K，饱和度降低10%，对比度降低5%。让所有画面感觉是"同一个下午的记忆"。

---

*导演备注：这个视频的力量不在画面本身。在画面和声音之间的张力——画面还在静止，但你在说"我还在折腾"。那个差距，就是这条视频后劲的来源。*
